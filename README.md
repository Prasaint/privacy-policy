# Privacy Policy – WiFi Tools: Scan & Speed Test

**App name:** WiFi Tools: Scan & Speed Test

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the WiFi Tools: Scan & Speed Test app ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you use it on your Android device. We designed the App to work on your device and to collect as little information as possible.

## 2. Summary

- We do not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
- We do not operate servers that receive your data. The App does not send any information to us.
- We do not show ads, and we do not use advertising or analytics services.
- We do not sell or share your personal information.
- Results of scans and tests are shown on your screen and are not saved. They are discarded when you close the App.

## 3. Information the App accesses on your device

To provide its tools, the App reads the following information from your device. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us.

- **Wi-Fi connection details:** network name (SSID), access point identifier (BSSID), signal strength (RSSI), frequency, channel and link speed of the network you are connected to.
- **Network addressing details:** your device's local IP address, subnet mask and gateway (router) address, and the type of connection (Wi-Fi, mobile data or Ethernet).
- **Nearby Wi-Fi networks:** the list of nearby networks that Android has most recently scanned, including network names, signal strength and channel.
- **Local network scan results:** when you run the Network Scanner, the App sends small connection requests to addresses on your local network. It shows the IP addresses, host names (when a device provides one), response times and, only if Android makes it available, the MAC addresses of devices that respond.

The App does not read your GPS coordinates or your location history.

## 4. Permissions the App requests

- **Location (ACCESS\_FINE\_LOCATION and ACCESS\_COARSE\_LOCATION):** Android requires this permission before any app can read the name of a Wi-Fi network or a list of nearby networks. The App uses it only for that purpose. The App does not collect, store or transmit your location.
- **Nearby Wi-Fi devices (NEARBY\_WIFI\_DEVICES, Android 13 and later):** required by Android to read nearby Wi-Fi scan results.
- **Wi-Fi state (ACCESS\_WIFI\_STATE) and network state (ACCESS\_NETWORK\_STATE):** used to read information about your current connection.
- **Internet (INTERNET):** used to run the speed test and network diagnostics described below.

You can deny or withdraw these permissions at any time in your device settings. Some features, such as showing the network name and nearby networks, will then be unavailable. The speed test and diagnostics continue to work.

## 5. Connections to third-party services

When you use the Speed Test and Network Diagnostics tools, the App makes network requests to the following public services. These requests are made directly from your device. As with any internet connection, the service receiving a request can see your device's public IP address and standard technical request information, and handles it under its own privacy policy.

- **Cloudflare** (speed.cloudflare.com, cloudflare.com, 1.1.1.1): used for speed test downloads and uploads, latency measurements, and the public IP address lookup. The uploaded speed test data is random data generated by the App and contains no personal information. Cloudflare Privacy Policy: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/
- **Google** (connectivitycheck.gstatic.com, 8.8.8.8): used to check whether the internet is reachable and to measure latency. Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **DNS lookups:** the Diagnostics tool asks your device's DNS resolver to look up public host names (for example example.com) to test DNS. These lookups are handled by your network's DNS provider.

The public IP address shown in Diagnostics is displayed to you only and is not stored or sent anywhere else.

## 6. Data we collect, store and share

We do not collect, store, transmit or share personal data. The App has no user accounts, no cloud storage, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Scan results, speed test results and signal history exist only in your device's memory while the App is open.

## 7. Data retention and deletion

Because the App does not store your information, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Uninstalling the App removes all of its data from your device.

## 8. Security

The App uses encrypted HTTPS connections for its internet requests. Local network probes are made only to addresses within your own network, and the results remain on your device.

## 9. Responsible use

The Network Scanner is intended for networks you own or are authorized to examine. Please use it only on such networks.

## 10. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
